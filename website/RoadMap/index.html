Where ABxAG is headed

Every era below is tracked in the open — specs and plans live in conductor/tracks/ on GitHub. Shipped means live in a release, not just merged.

Shipped

✓

Era 1 · Voice companion

Gemini Live voice chat, “Hey AB” wake word, reminders & alarms, quiet hours, auto-update that switches between any versions.

✓

Era 2 · Eyes

Real-time screen vision and OCR — she reads errors, documents and analytics straight from your screen. Screen text is always untrusted data.

✓

Era 3 · Hands

96 local PC tools: apps, files, mouse & keyboard, browser, clipboard, coding help — gated by confirmations, an emergency stop, and a permission board.

✓

Era 4 · Memory & mind

Local cognition runtime: episodic memory, goals, learned skills, curiosity and presence — she knows when to speak and when to stay quiet.

✓

Era 5 · Body

3D character engine (three.js): import any .pmx / .vrm / .glb / .fbx model, Character Studio, cloth physics, and a desktop-companion doll you can drag onto windows.

✓

Era 6 · Co-pilot

Dual-cursor: her own neon-red pointer works in the background while your mouse stays 100% yours — with a real OFF switch in Settings → PC control & safety.
