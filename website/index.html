Memory & initiative

Episodic memory, goals, learned skills and a polite sense of when to speak up — all processed locally.

Private by design

Your Gemini key is encrypted with Windows DPAPI and never reaches the web page. All AI calls go through your local backend.

You stay in charge

Emergency stop shortcut, permission toggles, virtual-cursor kill switch, a one-click stop on the HUD overlay, and “stop / cancel” always wins.

Dual-cursor co-pilot

Two cursors, one screen. You keep working — she works beside you with her own red pointer that never touches your mouse.

01

Your cursor stays yours

Her clicks go to background windows as messages — no focus steal, no cursor jumps, zero lag on your clicks.

02

She shows her work

A neon-red AI cursor glides to everything she touches, with a pill showing Active / Idle / Paused and her current task.

03

One switch to stop her

Settings → PC control & safety → Virtual AI cursor. Off means off — enforced in the backend, overlay hides too.

ABxAG — virtual cursor, live
# you browse YouTube while she fills a form in Notepad…
virtualFind  { "query": "Name field", "window_title": "Notepad" }
virtualUiAction { "action": "set_value", "value": "Hi from ABxAG" }
→ red cursor glides there · your mouse never moved · physical cursor untouched

HUD overlay

Her floating dashboard. Nine clickable widgets on an always-on-top window — including a live mirror of your loaded 3D character. Off by default, one keypress away.

01

Nine widgets, your pick

Status, mic, task, avatar, system, clock, emergency, conversation and quick actions — each toggles independently in Settings → Overlay.

02

She shows herself

The avatar widget mirrors whichever 3D character you loaded, with a glow while she speaks. Pause tasks, stop everything, or fire a quick action without opening the app.

03

Out of the way by design

Auto-hides for fullscreen games and apps, collapses to a compact mode, and respects the same kill switch as everything else.

ABxAG — HUD overlay
# press Ctrl + Alt + O anywhere, or…
Settings  →  "Overlay"  →  Enable overlay
# …and she floats above every window, clickable, dismissible, yours.
