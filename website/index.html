What she can do

🎙

Voice conversation

Natural low-latency voice chat powered by Gemini Live. Say “Hey AB” to wake her while the window is open.

👁

Screen vision

Share your screen and ask what she sees — errors, documents, anything. Screen text is always treated as untrusted data.

🖱

Real PC control

Opens apps, drives mouse & keyboard, manages files, chats and websites — with confirmation for risky actions.

🧠

Memory & initiative

Episodic memory, goals, learned skills and a polite sense of when to speak up — all processed locally.

🔒

Private by design

Your Gemini key is encrypted with Windows DPAPI and never reaches the web page. All AI calls go through your local backend.

🛑

You stay in charge

Emergency stop shortcut, permission toggles, and “stop / cancel / don’t do that” always wins.

Bring any character

ABxAG ships without a bundled model — import your own. Every format below loads out of the box:

.pmx.pmd.vrm.glb.gltf.fbx.obj

…or drop a .zip / folder containing one. Only use models whose licence allows your use.

⬇ Get ABxAG-Setup.exe Get a free Gemini key
  1. Install and launch ABxAG.
  2. Paste your free Gemini API key (stored encrypted on your PC).
  3. Press Import my character and pick a model file.
  4. Say “Hey AB”.
